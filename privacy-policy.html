Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

Your Privacy & Data Confidentiality

At Dhruthi Wellness, founded by Dt. Akhila Konakalla, M.Sc., we prioritize the protection, privacy, and clinical confidentiality of your personal and health information.

1. Introduction

Dhruthi Wellness ("we", "us", or "our") provides personalized clinical nutrition, fertility nutrition, PCOD/PCOS protocols, diabetes management, and metabolic wellness consultations led by Dt. Akhila Konakalla. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage in our nutrition programs.

By interacting with our website, booking a consultation, or sharing information with us, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

2. Information We Collect

To provide tailored dietary guidance and process your appointment requests, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, phone number, and email address provided during booking or inquiry.
  • Appointment Preferences: Preferred consultation dates, time slots, and selected nutrition programs (e.g. Fertility Nutrition, PCOD/PCOS Care, Diabetes Control).
  • Voluntary Health & Clinical Data: Dietary preferences, medical history notes, health goals, or laboratory reports voluntarily shared by you for personalized diet planning.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser session data, IP addresses, and device type to ensure optimal performance and security of our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate clinical, operational, and client communication purposes, including:

  • Scheduling and confirming 1-on-1 video or chat consultations with Dt. Akhila Konakalla.
  • Creating 100% customized, evidence-based nutrition and lifestyle plans.
  • Communicating appointment reminders, meal tracking updates, and consultation details via WhatsApp or email.
  • Responding to your inquiries and providing ongoing client support.
  • Maintaining accurate clinical records and fulfilling accounting or compliance obligations.

4. Data Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We respect the sensitive nature of health and personal data. We enforce strict client-practitioner confidentiality protocols:

  • No Data Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, trade, or commercialize your personal or health data to third-party advertisers or marketing agencies.
  • Restricted Access: Your clinical information is accessible solely to authorized wellness personnel led by Dt. Akhila Konakalla.
  • Secure Storage: Digital data is stored on secure databases and local encrypted storage equipped with modern security standards.

5. WhatsApp & Third-Party Communication

For convenience and real-time guidance, we utilize WhatsApp (`+91 8688963230`) as a primary communication channel for appointment setup and client follow-ups. WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted under WhatsApp's privacy framework.

We may also use secure cloud platforms (such as Supabase or Vercel infrastructure) strictly for website hosting, database backups, and processing form submissions securely.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies and browser local storage (`localStorage`) to enhance your user experience, such as saving your Light/Dark mode preference and managing session state. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal or health data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate or out-of-date information.
  • Request the deletion or removal of your records from our active database, subject to medical record retention rules.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

8. Policy Updates & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or clinical operations. Updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
